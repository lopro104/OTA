This is Android 16 QPR2 with April 2026 security patches

Notes:
==============================
- LineageOS-based source. Signed build.
- Make sure to be on sunfish firmware first.
- Clean flash is required if coming from March 2024 or older builds.
- Always dirty flash at your own risk. If your device bootloops, revert to an older build that boots.
- Includes LineageOS camera.

Device changes:
==============================
* Included an updater that works with my Unoffical builds!

Notable ROM changes:
==============================
* New lockscreen clocks
* Floating windows from A17 beta 3
* Dynamic Bar from AxionOS now available with help from crDroid
* Remembering section added to Evolver > About to honor our former
maintainer
* Trickystore is now integrated by default to the rom.
